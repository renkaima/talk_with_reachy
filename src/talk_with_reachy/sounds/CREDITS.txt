Sounds played by the take_break tool.

chime_start.wav and chime_end.wav are soft bell tones generated for this app (sine tones with a
gentle decay); they mark the start and the end of every break activity.

Music (activity "music"):

All three tracks are by Kevin MacLeod and were released into the public domain on FreePD.com
("free-to-use, Public Domain music downloads"; the site has since closed). The source MP3 files
were taken from the CC0-1.0-Music collection on GitHub (https://github.com/SoundSafari/CC0-1.0-Music,
folder freepd.com). Each file here is the first 60 seconds, with a 1 s fade-in, a 4 s fade-out,
loudness normalized to -16 LUFS, mono, 22.05 kHz.

calm_infinite_peace.wav       Kevin MacLeod, "Infinite Peace"
calm_kalimba_relaxation.wav   Kevin MacLeod, "Kalimba Relaxation Music"
calm_piano_magic_motive.wav   Kevin MacLeod, "Piano Magic Motive"
